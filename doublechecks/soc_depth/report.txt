9. THREE RECONSTRUCTIONS OF THE 1985 SUBSOIL — how much does the choice matter?
======================================================================================================================
 reconstruction sub20_40 profile_1985 rate_85_24 vs_measured
       measured   10.797       61.752      0.248       0.000
          shape   11.869       63.144      0.213       1.392
         static   12.656       64.098      0.188       2.346
        regress   13.846       65.398      0.155       3.645

  All three RAISE the 1985 subsoil, i.e. all three SHRINK the observed sink.
  Spread across the three: 2.25 Mg/ha on the 1985 level, 0.058 Mg/ha/yr on the rate
  For scale, the LITTER definition (section 7) moves the same rate by 0.109 Mg/ha/yr.

  => the reconstruction CHOICE is itself worth a large fraction of the effect, and
     'regress' is the most extreme because it inherits the 2006->2024 subsoil DECLINE
     and projects it backwards. That decline is the very thing in doubt.

  2006 and 2024 litter measurements are not comparable, or the layer is not input-limited.
